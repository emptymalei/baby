## Differences Between WHO and CDC Growth Charts

There is a big difference between the WHO and CDC growth charts[^cdc_who_growth_chart_diff].

| | WHO Growth Charts | CDC Growth Charts |
|------------------|-------------------|-------------------|
| Scope | International standard for infants and young children | children in the United States (1963 to 1994) |
| Condition | "how healthy infants and young children should grow under **optimal environmental and health conditions**"[^cdc_who_growth_chart_diff] | "Do not imply that the represented growth pattern is healthy"[^cdc_who_growth_chart_diff] |
